# Changelog

Releases are tagged `v1.x.y`. Sites that load `@1` get every 1.x release automatically, usually within a day.
Anything that would make a church change its settings goes in a new major version (`@2`) instead.

## 1.0.0 — November 2026

- First release: Full Circle Prayer from the WordPress plugin (1.13.0), for any website, with one script tag.
- Twelve movements, Simple and Full Prayer, timer, transitions, chime, notes, light and dark mode.
- Read and reflect passages open in a pop-out, loaded from the built-in World English Bible or King James Version.
- Church settings: name, logo, colors, Bible, starting time and experience, missionaries, local churches, countries, feedback email.
- Settings builder with live preview (`builder/`).
